# 🔭 IOAA Astrophysics Simulations & Labs

Welcome! This repository hosts a collection of interactive dashboards and web-based simulators designed to help students train for the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA). 

All math and physics engines load instantly, full-screen, and require zero setup or software installations.

## 🪐 Module 1: Vector Geometry & Celestial Kinematics

### 📐 dot-cross-product-interactive.html
A mathematically precise visualization sandbox designed to explore vector interactions in both 2D and 3D space. Students can manipulate, rotate, and animate two independent vectors, capital $\vec{A}$ and capital $\vec{B}$, to observe how their products shift dynamically.

* **2D Vector Projections:** See exactly how vector $\vec{A}$ projects onto vector $\vec{B}$ to determine the scalar Dot Product ($\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = ||\vec{A}|| ||\vec{B}|| \cos\theta$). The tool visually maps out when the projection is positive, negative, or zero (orthogonal).
* **3D Cross Product Space:** Rotate the canvas into 3D to see the orthogonal Cross Product vector ($\vec{C} = \vec{A} \times \vec{B}$) move dynamically. The engine tracks the changing area of the spanned parallelogram ($||\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|| = ||\vec{A}|| ||\vec{B}|| \sin\theta$) and enforces the Right-Hand Rule.
* **Astrophysics Application:** This exact geometry forms the framework for computing orbital angular momentum ($\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$) and tracking velocity component projections along line-of-sight vectors.

## 💡 Student Quick Start Guide
1. Click the **Launch Interactive Vector Simulator** link above.
2. The interactive panel will boot instantly in an edge-to-edge layout.
3. Use the sidebar sliders to change the magnitudes and spatial angles of vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$. 
4. Toggle the **2D/3D Projection** buttons to see vectors stretch, project, and recalculate their geometric matrices in real time.
