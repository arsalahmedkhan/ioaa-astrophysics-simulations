# 🔭 IOAA Astrophysics Simulations & Labs

Welcome to the **IOAA Astrophysics Simulations & Interactive Labs** repository! This project hosts a collection of web-based interactive visualizers, simulation sandboxes, and mathematical toolkits specifically designed to help students train for the **International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA)**.

---

## 🪐 Interactive Modules & Labs

### 📐 Module 1: Vector Geometry & Celestial Kinematics
* **[Dot & Cross Product Interactive Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/dot_cross_product_interactive.html)**  
  A 2D/3D interactive visualizer for computing orbital angular momentum ($\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$), orbital plane orientations, and line-of-sight radial velocity projections.

---

### 📈 Module 2: Observational & Dynamical Foundations
* **[Limits Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/limits.html)**  
  Explore point-source asymptotics and the foundation of the small-angle approximation ($\sin\theta \approx \theta$) vital for parallax, angular size, and telescope diffraction limits.
* **[Derivatives Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/derivatives.html)**  
  Visualize instantaneous rates of change to analyze exoplanet transit light curve ingress/egress slopes and spectroscopic radial velocity curves.
* **[Integrals Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/integrals.html)**  
  Integrate radial density profiles to compute total stellar mass, enclosed mass distribution, total radiant flux, and surface brightness profiles.

---

### ⚡ Module 3: Celestial Mechanics & Orbital Perturbations
* **[Maclaurin Series Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/maclaurin_series.html)**  
  Approximate asymmetric gravitational potential fields, solve series expansions for Kepler's equation, and calculate post-Newtonian orbital precessions.
* **[Taylor Series Lab](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/taylor_series.html)**  
  Model differential gravitational tidal forces, Roche lobe equipotential surfaces, and orbital perturbation approximations.

---

## 💡 Quick Start Guide
1. Visit the central landing hub at [**IOAA Astrophysics Simulations**](https://arsalahmedkhan.github.io/ioaa-astrophysics-simulations/).
2. Select any module to launch the interactive canvas in your browser.
3. Use the sidebar sliders and parameter controls to dynamically adjust physical parameters (such as vector magnitudes, step sizes, and integration bounds) in real time.
